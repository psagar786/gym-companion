## Prompt readiness rules

- Every canonical group has separate Start and Movement prompts under `PROMPT-MANIFEST.json`.
- Any inferred equipment, grip, stance, muscle, or joint-path field remains `needs-review`; it is not an approval.
- Prompts enforce square output, 512 x 512 PNG treatment, 10 percent safe margin, contained framing, no crop, no text, no logo, no white background, and no split view.
- Future exercise names must resolve through the canonical registry first; unmatched names produce a draft prompt that cannot be approved while required fields are ambiguous.
